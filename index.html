The four level metric hierarchy

Where you sit on the pyramid is where you sit on the org chart.
LEVEL 4 Valuation outcomes Revenue . Profit . Cash flow . Market cap
LEVEL 3 Asset metrics CLV . LTV:CAC . NPS . Brand consideration . Market share
LEVEL 2 Behavioural metrics Customer retention . Churn rate . Repeat purchase . CAC
LEVEL 1 Operational metrics CPM . CPC . CTR . ROAS . Impressions . Reach . Email open . Social engagement
Read this slowly. The lower the level, the easier the metric is to report and the less it tells you about whether the business is growing. The higher the level, the harder it is to measure and the more it matters. The smart CMO trades up the pyramid. The CFO already sits at the top.
